What I found

01

Your homepage and Before & After page each load about 15 MB.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the homepage loaded about 15 MB and its first content showed at 3.0 and 3.2 seconds (speed score 85 on both runs). The Before & After page loaded about 14.8 MB (score 87). Two of the gallery photos, IMG_5333 and IMG_3647, are 2000 by 2000 pixel PNG files of about 3.9 MB and 3.6 MB, and they are shown about 182 pixels wide. A third PNG, the eyebrow-removal photo, is about 2.9 MB. The test estimates that sizing the images properly would save about 12 MB. Your Contact page loaded about 1.4 MB. One test setup; field speed is unmeasured.

02

The testimonial video doesn't load.

The “Client Testimonial” section on your homepage and your Testimonials page both point to one video file, testimonial_04c6e5c2.mp4. In my load test, in three direct requests and in a browser check, the server answered with an error (500) instead of the video, and the video never started. The poster image loads. The file's header says it is about 76.7 MB, which is also very large for a web page. The Testimonials page has no other testimonial on it. I tried it repeatedly over about ten minutes; it may be a temporary server problem.

03

Your pricing page explains pricing two ways.

The page says “Pricing varies by tattoo size — contact us for an exact quote” and “Pricing is based on tattoo size and complexity”. Further down, and again on your FAQ page, it says “Our pricing structure is based on the actual amount of ink on your body, not the size of the area”. Your About page says “Pricing based on actual ink amount — not tattoo size”. I can't tell which one you want a visitor to believe.

04

Your 4.9-star review count isn't in the page code.

The homepage and the About page say 4.9 stars and over 100 Google reviews, and the homepage badge links to your Google Maps listing. None of the 10 pages I opened carries a rating or review in its page code. A tool that reads only the code can't see the rating, so the number exists only as text on the page.

05

The pages are heavy before they show anything.

In the same load test the homepage was blocked from responding for 130 to 180 milliseconds, the page document alone is 469 KB, including a 367 KB script block labelled “manus-runtime” (every page document I fetched is between 390 and 470 KB), and the test estimates 228 KB of the page's JavaScript goes unused. The server took 860 milliseconds to start answering, and the page is sent with a no-store setting so a returning visitor's browser can't reuse it. Your Videos page, which holds twelve links to Shorts, showed its largest content at 4.9 seconds (score 74).

06

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored the homepage 89, the Before & After page 89, the Videos page 89 and the Contact page 90 out of 100. The phone icon link in the header has no readable name. The gray “REAL RESULTS” and “CLIENT TESTIMONIAL” labels on white score 3.54 for contrast where 4.5 is expected. The homepage has no main-content landmark, a footer heading jumps a level, and the 16 photos in the homepage gallery share the same two descriptions, “Before laser tattoo removal progress photo in Las Vegas” and “After laser tattoo removal progress photo in Las Vegas”. The search-engine check scored every page I tested 100.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt says “Allow: /” and names your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 1,500 words of text and one clear heading (“Top Rated Laser Tattoo Removal Services in Las Vegas NV”), so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    An llms.txt file exists

    www.clearoutink.com/llms.txt loads as text. It lists your name, phone, address, hours, owner, services, financing and your key pages.

  • Found
    Business markup is thorough

    Your page code describes a local business with its address, map position, hours, phone, price range, founder and the cities you serve, and lists your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube profiles. The FAQ page has question-and-answer markup.

  • Gap
    No rating or review data in the markup

    The pages say 4.9 stars and over 100 Google reviews, but no page's code carries a rating or reviews.

  • Note
    Older addresses redirect, but search tools still hold older copies

    /faqs/ and /pricing/ send visitors to the current pages. In one search I ran, the preview text for those older addresses still showed a Cookeville, Tennessee location and a (931) phone number, which your current pages don't list. That is probably an older copy in the search tool's index.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
